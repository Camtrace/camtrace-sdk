## FFmpeg 7.1 — LGPL-2.1-or-later

`packages/web-video-decoder/src/mp4info.js`, `mp4info.wasm` and `mp4info.worker.js`
are a WebAssembly build of [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) 7.1 compiled with Emscripten,
plus a small CamTrace wrapper (`packages/web-video-decoder/ffmpeg-wasm/src/mp4info-wrapper.cpp`,
MIT).

- FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later
  (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html). This build is configured
  **without** `--enable-gpl` and **without** `--enable-nonfree`: it contains only FFmpeg's
  own libraries (libavcodec, libavformat, libavfilter, libavdevice, libavutil,
  libswresample, libswscale) with the `h264`, `hevc`, `mpeg4`, `aac` and `pcm_s16le`
  decoders, the `mov`/`matroska` demuxers and the `mp4` muxer. No GPL-only or non-free
  component is included.
- Corresponding source: the **unmodified** official release archive
  `ffmpeg-7.1.tar.bz2` (sha256
  `fd59e6160476095082e94150ada5a6032d7dcc282fe38ce682a00c18e7820528`), available at
  https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.tar.bz2. FFmpeg itself is not patched.
- Rebuilding and relinking: the complete build recipe — Dockerfile with the exact
  `configure` flags, Makefile with the Emscripten link flags, wrapper source — is in
  `packages/web-video-decoder/ffmpeg-wasm/`; `packages/web-video-decoder/scripts/rebuild-wasm.sh`
  regenerates the three artifacts (requires Docker). This lets you rebuild the module
  against a modified FFmpeg, as provided for by the LGPL.
- License texts: `COPYING.LGPLv2.1` and `LICENSE.md` in the FFmpeg archive.
